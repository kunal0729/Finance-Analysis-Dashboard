# Finance-Analysis-Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard that gives real-time insights into transactions, customers and risks, covering amounts, fees, tax and year-over-year performance across months, customer segments, states and transaction types.

****📌 Project Overview**

This dashboard turns 50,000+ raw banking transactions and 5,000 customer records into a single, filterable view of financial performance. It helps answer:

How much value is being transacted, and how does it compare with last year?
Which customer segments, states and transaction types drive the most value?
How much value is lost to failed or pending transactions?
How do fees and tax change over time?

**🛠️ Tools Used**

Power BI – dashboard design and interactivity
Power Query – data cleaning and transformation
DAX – KPI, year-over-year and dynamic metric calculations

**🔍 Key Insights**

Total value is ₹137.53M, up 1.41% on last year, while transactions fell 0.57%. Growth comes from larger tickets, as the average transaction value rose from ₹9.02K to ₹9.20K.
Fees (₹216.94K) and tax (₹39.04K) are flat year over year. Fees are only about 0.16% of the amount moved.
July is the peak month (about ₹12.5M) and September the weakest (about ₹10M).
85.4% of the value succeeds. Failed (₹14.4M) and pending (₹5.8M) transactions together hold about ₹20M.
Retail contributes 55% of the amount (₹76M). Premium follows with 18.7% and SME with 15.3%.
Maharashtra leads with ₹22M (about 16%). Karnataka and Gujarat follow, and the top three states make up about 39% of the total.
Loan EMI and Transfer account for about 55% of the amount. They are also the largest sources of fees and tax.
Card Payment, Withdrawal and Bill Payment average only about ₹5K per transaction, compared with roughly ₹14K for Loan EMI, Transfer, Deposit and Investment.
Female customers account for 52.8% of the value (₹72.6M) versus 47.2% for male customers.

**💡 Business Takeaways**

Reduce failed and pending transactions. About ₹20M in value is not completed, so improving payment success rates is a clear opportunity.
Protect and grow the Retail segment, while building up Corporate and Wealth, which together contribute only about 11%.
Focus on high-value states such as Maharashtra, Karnataka and Gujarat, and test campaigns in under-represented states.
Prioritize Loan EMI and Transfer services, since they drive most of the value, fees and tax.
Plan around seasonality. Run promotions in weak months such as February and September to smooth out demand.
